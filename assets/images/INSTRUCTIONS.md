# Images Required for Medical App
Please search for and download images matching these descriptions. 
Place them in this folder (`assets/images/`) with the exact filenames below.

## 1. Welcome Background
**Filename:** `welcome_bg.jpg`
**Description:** A high-quality, abstract medical background. Think blue/teal gradients, DNA strands, or a futuristic hospital corridor. Darker tones preferred for text readability.
**Style:** Modern, Clean, Technological.

## 2. Check-in Icon
**Filename:** `checkin.jpg`
**Description:** Close up of scanning a QR code, holding a smartphone, or a digital ID card.
**Context:** Used for the "Check-in" card.

## 3. Triage / Emergency
**Filename:** `triage.jpg`
**Description:** Hospital monitor displaying a heartbeat, or a blurred view of an emergency room.
**Context:** Used for the "AI Triage" card.

## 4. Doctor Planning
**Filename:** `doctor.jpg`
**Description:** A friendly doctor in a white coat, or a stethoscope on a desk/calendar.
**Context:** Used for the "Planning" card.

## 5. Pharmacy / Meds
**Filename:** `pharma.jpg`
**Description:** Clean blister packs, a robotic pharmacy arm, or a pharmacist holding a bag.
**Context:** Used for the "Coordination" card.

## 6. Dashboard / Analytics
**Filename:** `dashboard.jpg` (Optional, unused in current layout but good to have)
**Description:** Data visualization on a tablet or screen.
